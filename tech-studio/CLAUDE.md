STRICT SCRIPT RULES (owner, 2026-11-22), apply to every Short:
1. Irresistible curiosity hook in the first 1-2 seconds. 2. Never open with greetings, intros, channel names or explanations.
3. Create an unanswered question or mystery immediately. 4. Every sentence moves the story forward.
5. Short, natural sentences that sound great narrated. 6. Include a surprising fact, unexpected comparison or concrete example.
7. Keep curiosity alive throughout. 8. Give a satisfying mini-payoff; never empty clickbait.

PRE-RENDER VOICE GATE (owner, 2026-11-24, after myst01 sync/repeat errors cost a second render: "don't do the mistake again"): order is audio.py → (retake.py / reseg.py) → align.py → `python3 tools/check_vo.py <id>` → generator → render. check_vo.py MUST print PASS before ANY render: every line force-aligns to its own audio (no missing/extra words), no extra speech inside a line (repeats), no speech between lines (spill-over), caption words in order and inside their line (align.py now clamps). Batch Gemini takes (batch > 1) often mis-split lines: never trust verify_vo.py alone. After any retake, re-check the NEIGHBOUR lines (a retake replaces only its own window; spilled words of the neighbour can be lost or duplicated). Render once, after PASS; never re-render the same video for a fix the gate would have caught.
