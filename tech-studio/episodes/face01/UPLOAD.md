**Title:** Your phone checks your face with 30,000 invisible dots #shorts
Alt: How Face ID knows it's really you

**Description:**
On an iPhone, Face ID projects more than 30,000 infrared dots onto your face, invisible to your eyes. An infrared camera reads them and builds a 3D map, which is why a flat photo can't unlock it. A neural network compares that map with the one saved at setup, and checks that your eyes are open and looking at the screen. Apple puts the chance of a random person unlocking it at less than 1 in 1,000,000 (Touch ID: about 1 in 50,000). The catch: identical twins and look-alike siblings raise the odds. Your face data stays on the device, in its secure chip.
