# lst01 Short: teaser for ep07 "Is Your Phone Secretly Listening to You?"
Voice: Gemini Orus (owner request). Style: papercut + real logos/icons (mixed). ~35 s.

1. You talked about running shoes once. By the next morning, the ads were already waiting.  _(gripping, slightly spooked hook)_
2. So your phone must be listening, right?  _(suspicious, leaning in)_
3. Scientists tested over seventeen thousand apps. Not one was caught secretly recording your voice.  _(surprising reveal)_
4. But one company did hide secret whistles in TV ads, sounds only your phone could hear.  _(creepy twist, lower voice)_
5. And the real reason that ad found you is even creepier.  _(ominous, building curiosity)_
6. Your phone doesn't need to hear you at all. I decoded the four ways it finds you, in the full video. Tap the link.  _(confident payoff, inviting)_
7. Subscribe to TechDecoded with Max for more tech, decoded.  _(warm sign-off)_

Facts: 17,260 apps (Northeastern 2018); SilverPush audio beacons in TV ads (FTC warning letters, Mar 2016). Sources in ep07 script.md.
