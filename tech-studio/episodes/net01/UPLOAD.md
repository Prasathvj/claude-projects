**Title:** What if the internet disappeared? #shorts
Alt: One settings change took Facebook offline for 6 hours

**Description:**
In October 2021, a faulty configuration change on Meta's backbone routers took Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp offline for about six hours, and many of its internal tools went down too. Most of the internet doesn't travel through space: over 99% of data between continents runs through cables on the sea floor. In 2024, three Red Sea cables were cut, most likely by a ship's dragging anchor, and the UN estimates about a quarter of Europe–Asia data traffic was disrupted. The internet isn't a cloud — it's cables, routers and settings, which is why engineers build backup routes.
