## 1. Hook ★ Max (0:00) — Astra working by itself; promise the 'free Astra trap' payoff
1. OpenAI's new model can open apps on your computer, click through them, and finish a task while you watch.
2. It's called GPT-6 Astra. And right now, it's one of the most searched AI tools in India.
3. But most people hit the same wall. They open ChatGPT, look for Astra, and it simply isn't there.
4. So let's decode it: where Astra hides, how to switch it on, what it costs in India, and the free Astra trap. Stay for that one, it's catching a lot of people.

## 2. What Astra is (0:25)
5. GPT-6 Astra is OpenAI's top model, launched in September twenty twenty-six, built for computer work, coding and long, demanding tasks.
6. On November seventeenth, OpenAI upgraded it to GPT-6.2 Astra, and added a cheaper sister model called Sol.
7. Think of it like this. Sol is your fast, everyday assistant. Astra is the specialist you call for the hardest jobs.

## 3. FUN: Max can't find Astra ★ Max (0:45)
8. Our host Max had a big plan. Ask Astra to do his entire job, then take the afternoon off.
9. He opened ChatGPT. No Astra. He refreshed. Still no Astra. He blamed his Wi-Fi.
10. The Wi-Fi was fine. Astra just doesn't live where most people look.

## 4. Who gets it (1:05)
11. On the Plus plan, Astra sits inside two places: ChatGPT Work and Codex. Not the regular chat list.
12. On the Free and Go plans, it isn't included at all. Pro and business plans get it too, with much higher limits.
13. And even on Plus, there's a limit. OpenAI estimates roughly five to forty-five Astra messages every five hours, depending on the task.
14. So treat it like a precious resource. Big jobs only, not "write me a birthday message".
15. Before anything else, check which plan you're on in your ChatGPT settings. That decides everything.

## 5. Step by step ★ Max (1:40) — app windows, agent cursor, permission dialog, chat exchange in a phone
16. Here's how to switch it on. Step one: install the ChatGPT desktop app, on Windows or Mac, and update it.
17. Step two: at the top, switch from Chat to Work. Then pick Astra from the model menu.
18. Step three, the exciting part. Open Plugins, find Computer Use, install it and turn it on.
19. On a Mac, allow Screen Recording and Accessibility. On Windows, keep the app you want it to use visible on screen.
20. Step four: start your request with "at Computer" for desktop apps, or "at Chrome" for jobs in your browser.
21. Then say exactly what done looks like. For example: "Open my sales sheet, chart the monthly totals, and save it as a PDF on my desktop."
22. Before it touches any app, ChatGPT asks your permission. And you can watch every click, and stop it any time.
23. It can't see your screen or use an app unless you allow it. You stay in control.
24. One catch. A Work task started on the website or the phone app runs in the cloud. It can't reach the files on your own computer.

## 6. What people use it for (3:00)
25. So Max tried again, properly this time. And people are using Astra for jobs like these.
26. A folder of receipts, turned into one tidy expense sheet.
27. A simple website built from a sketch, then opened in the browser to check it really works.
28. Even 3D apps like Blender. Researchers built a whole test, called BlenderWorld, to measure how well AI agents create 3D scenes.
29. And for coders, pick Astra inside Codex, and it can work through a whole project, run the tests and fix what breaks.
30. It can also create real files, like documents, spreadsheets and presentations, not just text in a chat window.
31. And through the API, it can read about a million tokens at once. That's a few thick books in one go.

## 7. How good is it? (3:55)
32. So, how good is it? On a computer-use test called OSWorld two point oh, OpenAI reports Astra finished about seventy-three percent of tasks.
33. The previous top model managed about sixty-six percent, and took roughly seventy-five minutes per task, against about forty for Astra.
34. That's OpenAI's own testing, so treat it as a strong claim, not a guarantee.
35. OpenAI's launch chart even ranks Astra above Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2. Again, that's OpenAI's own chart.
36. And it isn't instant. Big tasks can take many minutes. It's for jobs you'd hand to an assistant, not quick questions.

## 8. FUN quiz: Astra or Sol? ★ Max (4:40)
37. Quick game. Astra or Sol? I give you a job, you pick the model.
38. Summarise a long email. Sol. Fast and cheap is perfect.
39. Go through fifty invoices on your computer and fill a spreadsheet. Astra. That's real computer work.
40. Fix a bug across a big codebase. Astra, or Sol on a budget. OpenAI says Sol nearly matches Astra on ready-to-merge code, at about a fifth of the cost.
41. A birthday message for your friend. Please. Use Sol. Save Astra for the hard stuff.

## 9. Price in India (5:15)
42. Now, the money question for India. ChatGPT Go costs three hundred and ninety-nine rupees a month, but it doesn't include Astra.
43. Plus is about one thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine rupees a month in India, including GST. That's the cheapest way to get Astra.
44. For many students that's a big monthly bill. If you mostly need help with writing and study, the free models or Go will do just fine.
45. Prices change, so always check the official pricing page before you pay.
46. For developers, the API costs eight dollars per million input tokens for Astra, and forty dollars out.
47. Sol is two dollars in and ten out. So Astra costs about four times as much.

## 10. The free Astra trap (payoff of the hook) ★ Max (6:00)
48. Now, the trap I promised. "GPT-6 Astra free" is one of the biggest searches in India right now, and scammers follow the searches.
49. Websites and APK files promising free, unlimited Astra are a classic way to steal your account and card details.
50. The only safe places are chatgpt.com, the official ChatGPT apps and OpenAI's own API. If a deal sounds too good, it is.

## 11. OpenAI's own warning (6:25)
51. And here's a warning from OpenAI itself. Astra is its first model rated "Critical" for cybersecurity, under its own safety framework.
52. That means it could find serious security holes with very little human help. So OpenAI watches its tasks, and can pause them mid-run.
53. OpenAI even admits Astra is harder to monitor than its previous model. That's why it watches it so closely.
54. For you, the lesson is simple. Give Astra only the apps and files a task really needs.

## 12. Five tips ★ Max (7:05)
55. Five quick tips before you start.
56. One. Give it the full picture. The goal, the files, and what finished looks like.
57. Two. Never let it near banking apps, passwords or payment pages.
58. Three. Break huge jobs into stages, and check after each one.
59. Four. Use Sol for small things, so your Astra limit lasts.
60. Five. Check its work. Even the smartest model can be confidently wrong.

## 13. Close ★ Max (7:50)
61. So, did Max get his afternoon off? Partly. Astra built his report while he watched, and he spent the time he saved checking it.
62. GPT-6 Astra isn't magic, and it isn't free. But if your work lives on a computer, it can save you hours.
63. Tell me in the comments: what's the first job you'd hand to Astra?
64. And subscribe to TechDecoded with Max for more tech, decoded.
