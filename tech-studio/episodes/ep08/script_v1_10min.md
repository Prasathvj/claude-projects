## 2. What Astra is
VISUAL: card "GPT-6 Astra · Sep 2026"; icons: computer, code, flask, briefcase; timeline Sep → Nov 17 (GPT-6.2); Sol vs Astra cards (everyday helper vs specialist).
5. First, what is it? GPT-6 Astra is OpenAI's top model, launched in September twenty twenty-six. OpenAI calls it its flagship for demanding, end-to-end work.
6. Its big strengths are using a computer, writing code, research and science, and long professional tasks.
7. And this month, on November seventeenth, OpenAI released an upgrade, GPT-6.2 Astra, along with a cheaper sister model called Sol.
8. Think of it like this. Sol is your fast, everyday assistant. Astra is the specialist you call for the hardest jobs.

## 4. Who gets it
VISUAL: plan cards Free / Go / Plus / Pro / Business: ticks and crosses; "Work + Codex" doors on Plus; GPT-6 Pro badge; limits counter 5–45 per 5 h with hourglass.
12. Here's who gets it. On the Plus plan, OpenAI includes GPT-6 Astra inside two places: ChatGPT Work and Codex, not the regular chat list.
13. On the Pro, Business and Enterprise plans, you also get GPT-6 Pro, a version that runs on Astra for the toughest questions.
14. And on the Free and Go plans, Astra isn't included. You'll still get OpenAI's everyday models, which are very capable for normal chats.
15. Even on Plus, there are limits. OpenAI estimates roughly five to forty-five Astra messages every five hours, depending on how heavy each task is.
16. So treat Astra like a precious resource. Use it for big jobs, not for "write me a birthday message".

## 5. How to use it, step by step ★ Max
VISUAL: UI mockups (labelled illustration): desktop app download, Chat → Work switch, model menu → Astra, Plugins › Computer Use toggles, macOS permission dialog, "@Computer" / "@Chrome" prompt box, example prompt, permission pop-up "Allow once / Always allow", live cursor.
17. Before anything, check which plan you're on in your ChatGPT settings. Free, Go, Plus or Pro. That decides everything.
18. Now, how do you actually use it? Step one: install the ChatGPT desktop app, on Windows or Mac, and update it to the latest version.
19. Step two: at the top, switch from Chat to Work. That's where Astra lives. Then pick Astra from the model menu.
20. Step three, the exciting part: Computer Use. Open Plugins, find Computer Use, install it, and turn on its switches.
21. On a Mac, you'll be asked to allow Screen Recording and Accessibility. On Windows, keep the app you want it to use visible on screen.
22. Step four: start your request with "at Computer" for desktop apps, or "at Chrome" for jobs inside your browser.
23. Then describe the task clearly. Which app, which file, and what "done" looks like.
24. For example: "Open my sales spreadsheet, make a chart of monthly totals, and save it as a PDF on my desktop."
25. Before it touches any app, ChatGPT asks for permission. You can allow it once, or always allow apps you trust.
26. And you can watch every click. If something looks wrong, you stop it.

## 6. What people use it for
VISUAL: Max's report; receipts → spreadsheet; sketch → website in browser; Blender cube + "BlenderWorld" test card; cloud vs local warning; Codex terminal with green tests; file icons doc/xls/ppt; "1M tokens ≈ a few thick books".
27. So Max tried again, properly this time. He asked Astra to turn his messy weekly numbers into a clean report.
28. Here are three jobs people are actually trying. Turn a folder of receipts into one tidy expense sheet.
29. Build a simple website from a sketch and a few lines of text, then open it in the browser to check it really works.
30. Even drive creative apps like Blender. Researchers have built a whole test, called BlenderWorld, just to measure how well AI agents can create 3D scenes.
31. One catch. If you start a Work task on the website or the phone app, it runs in the cloud. It can't reach the apps and files on your own computer.
32. For coders, the second door is Codex, OpenAI's coding agent. Pick Astra there and it can work through a whole project, run tests and fix what breaks.
33. Astra can also create real files: documents, spreadsheets and presentations, not just text in a chat window.
34. And through the API, it can read about a million tokens at once. That's roughly a few thick books in one go.

## 7. How good is it?
VISUAL: bar race OSWorld 2.0: Astra ~73% vs previous ~66%; stopwatch 40 vs 75 min; "OpenAI's own testing" stamp; Elo chart card; can't-do list.
35. So, how good is it? On a computer-use test called OSWorld two point oh, OpenAI reports Astra completed about seventy-three percent of tasks.
36. The previous top model managed about sixty-six percent, and needed roughly seventy-five minutes per task, against about forty for Astra.
37. That's OpenAI's own testing, so take it as a strong claim rather than a guarantee. Independent results vary from test to test.
38. OpenAI's launch chart for GPT-6.2 even ranks Astra above Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2. Again, that's OpenAI's own chart.
39. And what can't it do? It can't see your screen unless you allow it. It can't use apps you haven't approved.
40. And it isn't instant. Big tasks can take many minutes, so it's best for jobs you'd hand to an assistant, not quick questions.

41. Quick game. Astra or Sol? I give you a job, you pick the model.
42. Job one: summarise a long email. Sol. Fast and cheap is perfect.
43. Job two: go through fifty invoices on your computer and fill a spreadsheet. Astra. That's real computer work.
44. Job three: fix a bug across a large codebase. Astra, or Sol if you're on a budget. OpenAI says Sol nearly matches Astra on ready-to-merge code changes, at about a fifth of the cost.
45. Job four: a birthday message for your friend. Please. Use Sol. Save Astra for the hard stuff.

## 9. Price in India
VISUAL: rupee cards: Go ₹399 (no Astra), Plus ~₹1,999 incl. GST (Astra in Work + Codex), Pro "much more"; API cards $8/$40 vs $2/$10.
46. Now, the money question for India. ChatGPT Go costs three hundred and ninety-nine rupees a month, but it doesn't include Astra.
47. ChatGPT Plus is listed at about one thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine rupees a month in India, including GST. That's the cheapest way to get Astra, inside Work and Codex.
48. For many students, that's a big monthly bill. If you mostly need help with writing, study and everyday questions, the free models or Go will do the job just fine.
49. Pro plans cost a lot more and give much higher limits, plus GPT-6 Pro. Prices change, so always check the official pricing page before you pay.
50. And for developers, there's the API. After the November update, GPT-6.2 Astra costs eight dollars per million input tokens and forty dollars per million output tokens.
51. Sol costs two dollars in and ten dollars out. So Astra is about four times the price of Sol.

## 10. "Free Astra" scam warning
VISUAL: fake APK / website cards with SCAM stamp; padlock; official places: chatgpt.com, app, API.
52. Now, "GPT-6 Astra free" is one of the biggest searches right now. Be careful.
53. Searches for it in India have exploded, and scammers follow the searches.
54. Websites and APK files promising free, unlimited Astra are a classic way to steal accounts and card details.
55. The safe places are chatgpt.com, the official ChatGPT apps, and OpenAI's own API. If a deal sounds too good, it is.

## 11. OpenAI's own warning ★ Max
VISUAL: Vox clipping "first model rated Critical for cybersecurity"; shield + monitor eye that pauses a task; "harder to monitor" note; least-access lesson.
56. And here's the warning OpenAI gave itself. Astra is the first OpenAI model rated "Critical" for cybersecurity under its own safety framework.
57. In OpenAI's framework, that level means a model could find serious security holes, or plan an attack, with very little human help.
58. So OpenAI added extra safeguards. Monitors watch Astra's tasks and can pause or stop them mid-run, and OpenAI admits this can sometimes interrupt legitimate work.
59. OpenAI also said Astra is harder to monitor than its previous model. That's a big reason it watches it so closely.
60. For you, the practical lesson is simple. Only give Astra access to the apps and files a task really needs.

## 12. Five tips ★ Max
VISUAL: checklist cards 1–5 with icons (target, stages, bank-with-cross, Sol leaf, magnifier).
61. Here are five tips before you start.
62. One. Give Astra the full picture. The goal, the files, and what a finished job looks like.
63. Two. Break huge jobs into stages, and check its work after each stage.
64. Three. Never let it near banking apps, passwords or payment pages.
65. Four. Use Sol for small things, so your Astra limit lasts for the big ones.
66. Five. Double-check facts and numbers. Even the smartest model can be confidently wrong.
