**Description:**
In Japan, FANUC has run "lights-out" production since 2001, where robots build other robots and can run unsupervised for up to 30 days. In China, Xiaomi opened a dark factory in 2024 designed to make about 10 million phones a year. That sounds like the end of factory jobs — but people still design the products, program the robots and fix them when they break. The real question isn't robots versus humans: it's which jobs grow around them. Full video coming soon.
