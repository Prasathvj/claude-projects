**Title:** Your UPI money doesn't actually move (in those 2 seconds) #shorts
Alt: What really happens when you pay by UPI

**Description:**
When you pay by UPI, your app sends the request to its bank, which passes it to NPCI, the switchboard between every UPI bank in India. NPCI looks up the UPI ID (like name@bank), finds the bank account behind it, and asks your bank if you have the money. Your bank checks your PIN and balance and debits you; the shopkeeper's bank credits them. The banks settle the real money between themselves later, in batches. Scale: 24.5 billion UPI payments in August 2026 alone, about 9,000 every second.
