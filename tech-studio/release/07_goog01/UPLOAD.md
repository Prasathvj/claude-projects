**Title:** How Google knows what you're searching for #shorts
Alt: Google isn't searching the internet when you hit Enter

**Description:**
Google's autocomplete suggestions come from real searches people have already made, weighted by what's trending, what's common where you are, and your past searches. And when you hit Enter, Google isn't searching the whole web: crawlers have already visited public pages and followed their links, and everything goes into an index of hundreds of billions of pages (well over 100,000,000 GB). Then hundreds of signals, like language, location and device, decide what you see first.
