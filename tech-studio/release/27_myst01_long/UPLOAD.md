**Description:**
On 22 November 1987, millions of people in Chicago were watching the evening sports news. At 9:14 PM, the screen went black, and a man in a rubber mask appeared. Two hours later, he hijacked a second TV station, this time with sound. Nearly 40 years later, nobody knows who he was. We open the case file: how a TV station could be hijacked from the outside, the man who tried something similar on HBO and got caught, why this trail went cold, four clues, and the 2013 "zombie" emergency alert hack that shows the same weakness still exists. Plus one thing to check on your own Wi-Fi router tonight.
