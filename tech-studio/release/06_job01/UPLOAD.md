**Title:** AI writes code. So why are developer jobs coming back? #shorts

**Description:**
AI is writing more code than ever, yet US software job postings have been rising again since mid-2025. Indeed Hiring Lab found 71% of the increase (May 2025 to May 2026) came from senior roles, and 37% from jobs with "AI" in the title (the two groups overlap). Total software postings are still about 27.5% below pre-pandemic levels. The market isn't dead. It's picky.
