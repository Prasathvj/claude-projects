**Title:** Your AI just read a poisoned webpage. Now what? #shorts
Alt: 5 habits that stop your AI agent from hurting you

**Description:**
Your AI agent just read a poisoned webpage. What happens next depends on five habits:
1. Least privilege: if it only needs to read, don't let it send, pay or delete.
2. Approve anything you can't undo.
3. Keep browsing and work apart: the agent that reads random sites shouldn't be logged into your email.
4. Treat unknown files and plugins like email attachments.
5. Check what it hands you: a hijacked agent can just give you a poisoned link.
