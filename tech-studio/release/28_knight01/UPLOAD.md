**Title:** One missed server cost $460 million in 45 minutes #shorts

**Description:**
On 1 August 2012, Knight Capital, one of America's biggest stock traders, deployed new trading software. The new code reached only seven of its eight servers. The eighth still held "Power Peg", an old feature switched off since 2003, and the new code reused its old switch. At 8:01 AM the system sent 97 warning emails; nobody acted. When the market opened at 9:30, Power Peg woke up and fired orders nonstop: more than 4 million trades in 154 stocks. 45 minutes later, Knight had lost over $460 million (SEC figure; Knight reported about $440 million pre-tax). Within a week it needed a $400 million rescue, and in 2013 it was taken over by rival Getco. The lesson: delete dead code, and never deploy alone.
