**Title:** The biggest IPO ever — and its rivals paid for it #shorts
Alt: Claude's maker just broke the IPO record

**Description:**
Anthropic, the company behind Claude, priced its IPO at $83 a share — above its own $70–78 target range — and raised over $81 billion, the largest IPO on record, topping SpaceX. At that price it was valued at about $1.5 trillion. On day one the stock opened 34% higher at $111.50, then closed up 17% at $97.17.

The twist: alongside the IPO, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC bought about $26 billion of shares in a private placement at the same $83 price. The companies that sell the chips and cloud AI runs on are betting on it too.
