**Title:** GPS satellites have no idea where you are #shorts
Alt: GPS doesn't track you — your phone does the math

**Description:**
GPS satellites don't track you. They broadcast where they are and exactly what time it is, and your phone does the rest: it measures how long each signal took to arrive, multiplies by the speed of light to get distances, and combines at least four satellites to find you (the fourth fixes your phone's clock). The timing is so precise that relativity matters: GPS satellite clocks run about 38 microseconds a day fast, and without correcting for it, positions would drift by more than 11 km in a single day.
