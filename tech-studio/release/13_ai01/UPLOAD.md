**Title:** ChatGPT doesn't know what it's going to say next #shorts
Alt: How ChatGPT actually writes, one token at a time

**Description:**
ChatGPT doesn't plan its whole answer. It builds every reply one token at a time: a token is often a word, sometimes part of one (in English, roughly three quarters of a word). At each step the model gives every token it knows a probability, picks one, adds it, and repeats, hundreds of times for a long answer. It learned those probabilities from huge amounts of text. It isn't looking answers up; it's predicting them, which is why it can sound confident and still be wrong.
