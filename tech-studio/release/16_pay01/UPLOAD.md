**Title:** Why you shouldn't give AI your credit card yet #shorts
Alt: Would you hand your credit card to an AI?

**Description:**
New AI agents can browse stores and complete the checkout for you. Sounds great, until a shady website hides invisible text that tells your AI to ignore cheaper stores and buy an overpriced item. And because you gave the agent permission to use your wallet, your bank may treat the purchase as yours, not as fraud. Rule one: never give an AI your main card. Use a virtual card with a low limit.
