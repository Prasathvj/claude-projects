**Title:** Your AI moved your money. Who pays? #shorts
Alt: Your bank says your AI agent is YOUR problem

**Description:**
Your AI agent sends money from your account — without asking. Who pays? Citi's November 2026 update to its online banking terms says actions taken by a third-party AI agent using your login count as authorized by you, even if it ignores your instructions. Reports say other big banks are taking a similar line. The catch: the rule doesn't cover the bank's own AI tools.

Before you give any AI your bank login, ask: is it your bank's own tool, or someone else's?
