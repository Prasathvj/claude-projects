## Fact-check notes (read before approving)

Checked on 2026-11-17. Several of the sources are older government pages or reposts, so confirm the current wording.

| Claim in the script | Source |
| --- | --- |
| A closed fridge keeps food cold about 4 hours; after 4 hours without power, throw out perishable food | [USDA FSIS: keep your food safe during emergencies](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/emergencies/keep-your-food-safe-during-emergencies) |
| A full freezer holds about 48 hours if unopened; a half-full one about 24 | USDA FSIS (same page) |
| Keep fridge and freezer doors closed | USDA FSIS |
| Never taste food to judge it. When in doubt, throw it out | USDA FSIS |
| Plan one gallon of water per person per day for drinking and sanitation, at least three days | [Ready.gov: build a kit](https://www.ready.gov/kit); older Ready.gov pages give three days as the baseline |
| Never run generators, grills or other fuel-burning devices inside the home, garage or carport, or near doors, windows and vents | [CDC: avoiding carbon monoxide poisoning](https://www.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/psa-toolkit/avoiding-carbon-monoxide-poisoning.html) |
| A fan will not stop carbon monoxide building up | FEMA |
| Battery-backup carbon monoxide detector on every level | [Ready.gov: power outages](https://www.ready.gov/power-outages) |
| Unplug appliances and electronics; power can return with a surge | Ready.gov: power outages |
| Leave one light on so you know when power returns | [Ready.gov kids: power outages](https://www.ready.gov/kids/know-the-facts/power-outages); Red Cross |
| Kit: flashlight, spare batteries, battery radio, water, non-perishable food, phone charger with backup battery | Ready.gov: build a kit |
| Carbon monoxide has no smell and no colour | General public-health fact |

Left out on purpose, because I could not confirm them:
- A "safe distance" for a generator. Sources disagree (20 feet in one place, 25 in an older CDC page), so the script says "well away from doors, windows and vents" and gives no number.
- "Use flashlights, not candles." It is standard advice, but I did not verify it against an official page.
- Anything about heating, medical equipment or refreezing food.
