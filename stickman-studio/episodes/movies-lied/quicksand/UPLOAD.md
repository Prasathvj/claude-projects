Can quicksand really swallow you whole? No. Here is the truth about quicksand, how to escape quicksand, and what to do if you get stuck.

Movies show people sinking out of sight, but real quicksand lets your body float, usually only to about waist depth. What actually hurts people is panic, exhaustion and a rising tide.

If you are stuck in quicksand:
1. Stay calm and stop struggling. Movement makes the sand more liquid.
2. Lean back and spread your weight.
3. Slowly wiggle your legs to let water into the gaps.
4. Never walk on wet, loose or unusually smooth ground near a tide, and hike with a buddy.
