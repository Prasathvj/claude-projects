Caught in a thunderstorm outside? Do not hide under a tree. Movies make a tree look like shelter, but standing under a tree is the second leading cause of lightning casualties, according to the US National Weather Service.

Lightning safety in a thunderstorm:
1. If you hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck. Lightning can reach up to 10 miles from a storm.
2. Get inside a sturdy building, or a hard-topped car with the windows up. No place outside is safe.
3. Stay inside until 30 minutes after the last thunder.
4. Never shelter under an isolated tree.
