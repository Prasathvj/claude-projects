Title: Stuck in an elevator? Do this.
Description:
Stuck between floors? Don't panic. Check the stop button, press the call or alarm button, use your phone or bang on the door if nobody answers, stay inside, and never pry the doors open.

General information, not professional advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Sources: Otis (what to do if your elevator stops), Northeastern University elevator failure guide, University of Toronto Facilities, Technical Safety BC, Elevator World (elevator safety systems).
