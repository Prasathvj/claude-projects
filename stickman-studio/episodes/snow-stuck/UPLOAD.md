Description:
Car stuck in snow? There is one mistake that makes it much worse: spinning the wheels. Here is what to do instead.

1. Don't spin the wheels. It digs you in deeper.
2. Clear the snow from around the tires and from under the car.
3. Move a little at a time: back up, then drive forward over packed snow.
4. Still stuck? Call for help and stay with your car unless safety is clearly close by.
5. Clear the tailpipe before you run the engine: a blocked pipe can let carbon monoxide into the car.
6. Run the engine only 5 to 10 minutes an hour, with a window cracked a little.
