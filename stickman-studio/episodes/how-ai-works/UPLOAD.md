## SHORT (vertical, 70 s): out/short.mp4
Title: How does AI actually work? (in 60 seconds)
Description:
Ask an AI anything and it answers. Here is how it works: it guesses the next word. Text is chopped into tokens, billions of tiny dials are nudged during training, attention reads each word in context, and answers are written one piece at a time. People rate answers so it learns to be helpful, but it aims for text that sounds right, not text that is checked, so it can be confidently wrong.

The probabilities on screen are illustrative, not real model numbers.

Sources: Vaswani et al., "Attention Is All You Need" (arXiv 1706.03762); Google Machine Learning Crash Course; 3Blue1Brown, "Attention in transformers"; survey on hallucination in large language models (arXiv 2311.05232).
