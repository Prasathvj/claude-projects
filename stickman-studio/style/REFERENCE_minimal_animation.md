# Reference: minimal-animation stickman video (https://youtu.be/Qk84gzPlvuk)

Analysed with vidIQ on 2026-11-17. It is a 14-minute 16:9 survival story ("30 days of darkness"), not a Short, but the
style is exactly the "minimal animation that never alters the character" the channel owner wants.

## What it does
- Full-screen illustrations, 120+ distinct pictures in 14 minutes, about 7 seconds each (2 to 12 s).
- Characters are 100% consistent in proportions and line weight, and each is told apart by one accessory
  (yellow beanie, red cap, purple cardigan, mechanic jumpsuit).
- Movement is minimal: very slow constant push-ins and gentle lateral pans; brief shake on warnings; looping snow,
  breath vapour and candle flicker; a few simple pose swaps (2 to 3 frames) or one arm rotating; occasional eye blink.
  No lip-sync, no face morphing.
- Hard cuts more than 85% of the time. Dip to black for day changes. Slide or fade inside infographic boards.
- No burned-in subtitles. Title cards: white bold sans-serif on black ("DAY ONE").
- Infographic boards on stark white: red "no" circle with slash, green check marks, red arrows, hand-drawn clipboards.
- Voice: male, General American, about 125 to 135 words per minute, calm and documentary.
- Audio: low ambient drone; sparse foley hits (radio static, wind, drip, generator hum, candle crackle); voice about
  -14 to -16 LUFS with music 18 to 24 dB below the voice.
- Look: cold blue, charcoal, slate; warm amber only for safety (candle, flashlight, bulb); heavy dark vignette.
- Hook: states the premise in the first 3 seconds, then a striking image by 8 s. Ending: calm recap and a symbolic close-up.

## How we apply it
- Shorts: dedicated 9:16 pictures, slow push-ins, short shake on impacts, particles, hard cuts, dip to black at the start and end.
- Keep captions on Shorts (word-by-word), because Shorts are watched muted; the reference has none.
- Red X, green check and arrows as animated overlays added in code, so they never cover the character.
- Differentiate characters by one accessory (the headlamp only when the scene is dark).
- Optional: a second pose for key beats, cut between two pictures, instead of redrawing limbs.
