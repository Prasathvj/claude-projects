# Channel style sheet (v1)

Reference image for every shot: `character-sheet-v1.jpg` (attach it in Flow each time).

## Characters
- Hero: black stick body and limbs, large white round head, dot eyes, short line mouth, white mitten hands, white shoes, orange headlamp on a black strap, worn olive-green backpack.
- Dog: small scruffy white dog, dot eyes, black stick legs, red bandana.

## Look
- Uniform solid black outlines, flat cel shading, light grunge texture on surfaces.
- Muted cold palette (slate blue, steel grey, dusk purple). Orange is the only strong warm colour: headlamp, its glow, small light sources.
- Detailed perspective backgrounds; characters small to medium in frame.
- No text, no weather particles in the image (snow, rain and lightning are added in editing).
- 16:9, highest resolution Flow offers.

## Prompt ending (append to every shot)
2D cartoon, clean graphic-novel style, uniform solid black outlines, flat cel shading, muted cold colours with orange as the only warm accent, detailed background. Characters exactly as in the attached character sheet. No text, no falling snow or rain. 16:9.

## Editing rules
- Shot length 4-7 s, 2-3 s in action beats; hard cuts.
- Slow push-in or pan on every shot; weather overlay and light flashes added in code.
- Tip cards: dark navy background, orange uppercase header, white body text, one takeaway line.
- Chapter cards: black background, white uppercase text.
- Narration: second person, present tense, deep calm male voice. No running captions.
- Sound: low drone music plus weather and object sound effects.
